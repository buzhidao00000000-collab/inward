# Dynamic sound artwork

Rain, waves, stream, forest birds, wind, and summer-night cards embed locally downloaded scene photographs from ../scenes/sources.json and add original SVG loop effects (rain, ripples, drifting birds, wind lines, firefly lights). They are animated SVG images, not downloaded video footage. White/pink noise use original animated SVG grain and waveform graphics.

The fireplace card is an original animated SVG hearth/flame illustration. All nine source animations are original SVG effects. Their `.gif` versions are 320×180, 10 fps, six-second frame renders produced locally with Chromium and FFmpeg for use in the sound-selection wheel. They are not downloaded live-action footage. The static `.still.svg` posters are retained.

The former Aurora, Wildflower sky, and Moonlit scenery have been replaced by real Unsplash photographs; see ../scenes/sources.json for photo IDs, authors, acquisition paths, and hashes. Firefly scenery remains a sourced forest photograph with original animated light points. Existing sound-card photographs remain embedded locally in their SVG assets.
