# Meditate scenery and sound wheels

The Scenery button opens nine themes: starry lake, aurora, firefly forest, seaside, misty forest, rainy lake, waterfall, wildflower sky, and moonlit landscape. The blurry desert image has been removed. Replacement photographs for the illustrated/non-natural scenes remain pending access to stock-photo sources; do not describe them as replaced yet. Artwork sources and processing details are in `scenes/sources.json`. Aurora and moon imagery are sourced illustrations. The firefly scene overlays animated points on a sourced forest photograph; it is not presented as a firefly photograph.

The Audio button opens nine corresponding animated cards: rain, ocean waves, stream, forest birds, wind, white noise, pink noise, summer night, and fireplace. Choosing a sound starts that recording. The floating playback/volume panel has been removed. The existing Start/End meditation button starts or stops the chosen recording; before a sound is chosen, Start opens the sound wheel. Use device volume controls for loudness. Sound credits are linked from the sound wheel. Leaving Meditate pauses playback. Initial page load never plays audio. Playback errors are announced to assistive technology; Start retries playback.

Both wheels use the same upright, spring-driven, infinite looping interaction, supporting drag, click, scroll and keyboard navigation. Opening one closes the other. Escape, outside clicks and leaving the tab close the wheel. The Start/End button remains usable after outside-click dismissal.

All photos, animated images and audio are local assets. `sound-art/SOURCES.md` distinguishes the original animated SVG effects and fireplace SVG effects. `sounds/SOUNDS_LICENSING.md`, `sounds/sources.json` and the user-accessible `sounds/credits.html` retain the recording authors, sources and licenses. Audio is AAC for phone compatibility, using up to 40 seconds of each source and a one-second tail/head crossfade before encoding. HTML audio loop timing still depends on the device.

`scene-picker.js` and `scene-picker.css` are embedded in both main HTML entry pages; synchronize these copies. Current-instance Chromium mobile validation covers all nine remaining scenery changes, real sound playback, Start/End, tab cleanup, absence of the volume popup, and the white-noise static/selected artwork on both main entries. Canvas pixel comparisons confirm the white-noise poster remains unchanged before selection. This revision does not revalidate all nine audio decoders. Headless validation cannot substitute for listening to audio output or testing real iOS Safari.

Sound card SVGs have separate `.still.svg` posters. Unselected cards and closed wheels always use posters; only the selected card in an open wheel loads its animated SVG. Reduced-motion mode keeps the still poster even when selected. Both main pages were checked with screenshot comparisons before and after selection.
